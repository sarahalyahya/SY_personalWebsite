Sarah Al Yahya is a creative technologist and researcher based in Amman. Her practice explores internet cultures, online communities, and how users appropriate digital tools to resist, organize, and imagine alternative worlds, with a focus on vernacular digital practices in Palestine and the Arab world. She experiments with software art, spatial media, and physical computing. Her work has been exhibited in the US, Austria, Spain, the Netherlands, and the UAE.
